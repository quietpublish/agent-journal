# {{PROJECT}} journal

> Where we've been, where we are. Not what changed — why it changed.

This journal captures the decisions, pivots, and lessons that git history can't.
Each entry is written at the moment of realization, not reconstructed later.
Append-only, newest first. Entries are never edited; a wrong entry gets a
`[correction]` entry above it that names what it supersedes.

How to read: `docs/BELIEFS.md` is the short current picture. This file is the
evidence. Find entries with `docs/JOURNAL_INDEX.md` (line ranges) or the
`/journal search` skill. Terms in bold are defined in `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

How to write: `/journal` drafts an entry; a human approves; the writer script
appends. Format and voice rules live in the journal plugin's SCHEMA.md.
