## Journal

1. **Read `docs/BELIEFS.md` first, then the top three entries of `docs/JOURNAL.md`.**
   That is the project's memory: decisions, pivots, lessons git can't hold.
   Deeper: `docs/JOURNAL_INDEX.md` gives line ranges; `/journal search <query>`
   ranks entries by meaning.
2. **Add an entry with `/journal`** when a decision is made, a lesson is learned,
   or a dogfood surprises you. Not for routine progress. The human approves
   before the writer appends.
3. **Never edit an entry.** A wrong one gets a `[correction]` entry that names
   what it supersedes.
4. **Define terms.** A new project term goes in bold on first use and gets a
   line in `docs/GLOSSARY.md` in the same change. Use the glossary's spelling.
5. **Write for a cold reader.** Title states the finding. First sentence is the
   lesson. Under 250 words. Evidence by path or commit.
